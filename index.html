Intimidation-Free Since Day One

BFF Bikes was founded by Vanessa and Annie, who recognized the need for a supportive and encouraging environment for cyclists of all abilities.

In 2020, Gillian Forsyth took the helm. With a background in endurance sports and a passion for community, Gillian has continued the mission of making BFF Bikes the kindest shop in Chicago.

"Our mission is to make sure every customer walks away with the right bike for them."
